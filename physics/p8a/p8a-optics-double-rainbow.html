Why is 霓 Inverted & Dimmer?

核心原理解析
1. Bottom Entry 底部入射

While 虹 (Primary) enters the top of a raindrop, 霓 (Secondary) comes from sunlight hitting the bottom of the drop and refracting upward.
主虹光线从水滴上半部射入；而副虹（霓）的光线从水滴下半部射入并向上折射。

2. Two Internal Reflections 两次内反射 (色序反转)

Inside the drop, light reflects twice before exiting. Each reflection flips the ray order—just like looking in two mirrors—so Red exits steeper (~51°) and Violet exits flatter (~53.5°).
光线在水滴内经历两次反射，如同照两次镜子发生交叉翻转，导致红光与紫光的出射角度顺序与主虹完全相反。

3. Why High Drops Send Violet 为什么霓是“外紫内红”？

To reach your eye, droplets higher in the sky (~53.5° elevation) send Violet down to you, while lower droplets (~50.8°) send Red. Thus, Violet is on top!
高处水滴（仰角约 53.5°）把紫光射入人眼，低处水滴（约 50.8°）把红光射入人眼，因此我们看到外紫内红。

4. Dimmer Glow & Alexander's Dark Band 亮度更暗与暗带

At each reflection, ~95% of light escapes out of the drop and only ~5% reflects inside. Two reflections leave 霓 much fainter than 虹, separated by Alexander's Dark Band (42°–51°) where no internally reflected light exits.
每次内反射都会损失大量光能，因此“霓”比“虹”暗淡得多；两者之间（42°–51°）没有反射光射出，形成著名的亚历山大暗带。
