Two Raindrops Create One Stacked Rainbow
Sunlight hits millions of falling raindrops at once. A higher raindrop sends its 42.0° red beam straight to your eye (while its violet beam passes over your head). A lower raindrop sends its 40.1° violet beam to your eye (while its red beam hits the ground)—stacking red above violet.
